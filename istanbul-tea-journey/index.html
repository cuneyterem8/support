Istanbul Tea Journey · Support

Carry a bowl of tea along Istanbul’s uneven streets. Balance it on the cart, stop for your neighbours, and blend their tea with hot water.

Two ways to travel

Free Play is an endless road: keep the bowl balanced, save your tea and aim for a new personal record. Tea Route has ten chapters with customer stops, marked parking areas, individual tea-and-water recipes, and later temperature, arrival-time and return-glass goals.

How to serve tea

Brake early and stop with the cart touching the yellow parking area. Pour toward the customer on the left or right. Choose tea or hot water, use the balance slider and follow the displayed mixture range, fill level and temperature. You may finish a partial service; the customer’s payment reflects what you delivered.

Gold and Add Tea

Add Tea spends 50 gold and adds 50 percentage points to the bowl, up to 100%. You need enough gold and an active journey. Normal score rewards and customer payments do not require watching ads. There are no ads or advertising reward multipliers.

Optional iOS purchase

The Shop offers a repeatable, consumable 10,000 Gold pack. Each purchase is a separate payment, not a subscription. The intended US price is $4.99; the actual price and currency shown before purchase come from your App Store storefront. The browser edition does not take real-money payments.

A purchase has not appeared

With an internet connection, reopen the app and Shop to retry an unfinished delivery. An already credited transaction is not credited again. If it still fails, contact support with the app version, device model, iOS version and a brief issue description. Do not uninstall the app while investigating a wallet problem.

Saves, language and sound

Progress, gold, personal records, chapter progress, appearance and settings stay on this device. English is the first-launch language; Settings lets you choose English or Turkish, light or dark appearance, and separate music and sound levels. There is no game account, Game Center leaderboard or developer cloud wallet.

Before reinstalling or clearing data

Deleting the app or its saved data can remove progress and the gold wallet. Completed consumable purchases are not restored as a fresh balance after reinstalling, and the wallet does not sync between devices. Unfinished Apple transactions can still be retried. Request an Apple purchase refund if appropriate; Apple handles the request.

Report a problem

Email the contact below. Include the chapter or game mode and what happened; a screenshot may help. Redact private purchase details and never send passwords, verification codes or payment-card information. For a performance issue, also mention the selected graphics quality.
