Istanbul Tea Journey · Terms

Game-specific information about playing Istanbul Tea Journey, optional gold purchases and local saves.

Playing and optional services

The core game can be played offline without creating a game account. Apple purchases and support links need an internet connection and may be unavailable during service interruptions. The game contains no advertising, rewarded-video multiplier or Game Center leaderboard.

Gold and purchases

Gold is an in-game resource used for Add Tea. It cannot be transferred or redeemed for cash. The optional 10,000 Gold pack is consumable and may be purchased repeatedly; each purchase needs Apple authorization. It is not a subscription. StoreKit supplies the actual storefront price and currency before payment. Purchased gold does not have a scheduled expiry.

Your local wallet

Gold, completed chapters, records and settings are stored locally. There is no developer cloud wallet or cross-device balance transfer. Deleting the app or saved data can remove them. Completed consumable purchases do not create a new balance after reinstalling. Keep the app installed while a support case about missing gold is investigated.

Delivery problems and refunds

The app retries unfinished Apple transactions and records credited transaction identifiers to prevent duplicate rewards. Reopen the app and Shop online if delivery is interrupted, then contact support if the issue remains. Apple Report a Problem handles eligible Apple purchase refund requests under Apple’s process and applicable law.

Use and updates

Use the game and purchase features as intended. Do not redistribute proprietary game code or assets without the relevant permission. Features and these notes may be updated with future versions. Important changes will be reflected in the available policy information. The privacy page explains game, email and website data handling separately.
