Istanbul Tea Journey · Privacy

Your journey stays on your device. This policy explains the game, optional Apple purchases, support email and this support website separately.

Publisher and scope

Publisher: Mihriban Erem. Support and technical operations: Cüneyt Erem. This policy covers Istanbul Tea Journey / İstanbul Çay Yolculuğu and its support pages.

Data kept in the game

The game has no player account, chat, user-generated content, advertising SDK, remote analytics or runtime AI service. We do not send your gameplay saves to a developer server. Progress, records, preferences, chapter history, gold and purchase transaction identifiers remain on the device. Customer names are fictional game characters, not a request for your name or address.

Controls and permissions

If you choose tilt controls, supported devices may request motion permission. Readings are used locally to control the game and are not sent to us. The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location. Device language/region preferences are not GPS location.

Apple purchases

The iOS Shop uses StoreKit for the optional consumable 10,000 Gold pack. Apple processes payment and purchase authorization. The app uses verified product, quantity and transaction information locally to deliver gold once and retry unfinished delivery. We do not receive your payment-card details through the game. Apple’s privacy policy explains Apple’s processing.

Storage and deletion

There is no developer account or cloud wallet to sync between devices. Removing the app or its data can remove local preferences, records, progress and gold. Completed consumable purchases do not refill a new installation. Avoid deleting the app when troubleshooting a purchase. The browser edition uses browser storage and has no real-money checkout.

When you email support

Your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are processed to answer your request and resolve the issue. Send only what is needed. Support correspondence is kept only as needed for support and applicable record obligations. You can contact the address below about access or deletion of that correspondence, subject to applicable law.

Visiting these support pages

These are static GitHub Pages pages with no embedded analytics, ad trackers or client-side scripts. GitHub logs technical requests, including IP addresses, for security when its Pages service is visited. That website hosting is separate from in-game saves. GitHub’s privacy statement explains its handling. Outbound Apple links and your email provider have their own practices.

Changes and privacy questions

This policy is updated when the app’s data handling changes. The contact below handles privacy questions as well as game support. The game has no registration form that asks children or other players for personal information.
