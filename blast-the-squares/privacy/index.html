Privacy

Blast the Squares · Privacy

This policy covers Blast the Squares, including the current TestFlight version, and its support pages. Publisher: Mihriban Erem. Support and technical operations: Cüneyt Erem.

Data on your device

The app stores the coin balance, settings, game progress, scores, and purchase transaction identifiers on your device. Transaction identifiers prevent duplicate coin delivery. The publisher does not receive these game records through a developer-operated server. Resetting the game clears progress and coins, while delivered transaction identifiers are retained locally to prevent duplicate delivery. Removing the app removes its local game data.

Language and location

The app reads the iPhone region setting to choose its initial language. A manual language choice is saved. The region code is not saved. We do not request GPS access or collect precise location. The purchase price is supplied by Apple for your App Store storefront.

Game Center

When you use Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center account and submitted scores. The app receives the player identifier, display name, and leaderboard scores needed for this feature and keeps local per-player score records and pending submissions. No separate game account is created. See Apple Game Center privacy information.

Your Game Center nickname and submitted leaderboard scores may be visible to other players through Game Center.

Apple purchases

Apple processes payments. The app uses verified transaction identifiers, product identifiers, and quantities to deliver coins once and retry unfinished delivery. We do not receive your payment card information. Apple handles its own payment and account records under Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising and tracking

AdMob-enabled iOS builds use Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices. Earlier TestFlight builds with the local demo screen do not use advertising SDKs. Builds labelled Test ads request Google's test advertisements; test mode does not mean the SDK makes no network requests. AppLovin is not integrated.

Google may process your IP address (including approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash/performance information for advertising, measurement, diagnostics and analytics. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps. Google manages its own retention and privacy controls.

Where required, privacy messages let you accept, refuse or manage applicable processing choices. Revisit Settings → Ad privacy choices (or Check privacy options). Apple's tracking permission is separate: declining it does not prevent play, purchases, or eligibility for an available rewarded ad. You can change tracking permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ad availability depends on your choices, region and the network.

The app does not send your Game Center identity, scores or coin balance to Google. A random ad-show identifier and round identifier are saved locally to retry earned rewards without paying twice. These local receipts are removed after delivery or when the app is deleted. We do not operate a game analytics server. Region-specific rights requests may be sent to support; we cannot retrieve device-only game records from a server.

Support messages and retention

If you email support, Cüneyt Erem receives your email address and the content and attachments you choose to send, using Gmail. This information is used to handle your request and related necessary records, not for advertising. Messages are kept while needed for the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Ask about access, correction, or deletion at cuneyterem8@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, verification codes, or full card information.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They do not contain our own tracking scripts, advertising, or analytics. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security, as explained in GitHub's privacy statement. Website hosting records are separate from the game's local data.

Audience and changes

The game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. Policy changes will be published on this page with an updated date. Any permissions required for new processing will be requested separately.
