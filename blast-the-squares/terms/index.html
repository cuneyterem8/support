Coins and purchases

Gold is a virtual in-game resource, has no cash value, and cannot be transferred or redeemed for money. The 1,000 Gold pack is optional, consumable, and may be purchased repeatedly. It is not a subscription. Payment authorization, regional prices, and purchase handling are provided by Apple.

Local saves

Coins and progress are stored on your device. Deleting the app or resetting progress removes them. Game Center keeps supported leaderboard scores, not your coin balance. Completed consumable purchases do not refill the balance after a reinstall.

Reopen the app and the shop with an internet connection to retry an unfinished purchase delivery. For continuing problems, email cuneyterem8@gmail.com. Refund requests for Apple purchases are handled through Apple Report a Problem, subject to Apple's process and applicable law.

Ad duration, availability and closing controls are managed by the network. Test ads do not generate advertising revenue.
