Support

Blast the Squares · Support

Get help with gameplay, coins, purchases, and Game Center.

Contact

Support and technical operations: Cüneyt Erem. Email cuneyterem8@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment card details.

How to play

Drag a piece onto the board. Tap a piece to rotate it before placing it. Complete rows or columns to clear space. Boosters spend the coin amount shown in the game only when used on a valid target.

Coins and local progress

Coins, settings, and saved games stay on your device. The game has no developer-operated cloud save or separate registration. Deleting the app or resetting progress removes the local balance. Coins cannot be transferred between devices through Game Center.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game can submit and display your Classic and Daily best scores. You can play without Game Center. Check iPhone Settings → Game Center if your account is unavailable. Each leaderboard stores the best single run for its mode; the Daily leaderboard is not a daily-reset competition.

The 1,000 Gold purchase

This is a repeatable consumable purchase, not a subscription. Apple handles payment. The price comes from your App Store country and does not change when you change the game language. After a completed purchase, 1,000 gold is added once. If delivery is interrupted, reopen the app and visit the shop while online.

Missing coins or refunds

If the balance is still wrong, contact support with the purchase date and a redacted receipt. Delivered consumable coins are not restored after deleting the app. You can request an Apple purchase refund at Apple Report a Problem; Apple decides the request.

Advertisements

The current version has a demo reward screen; AppLovin and AdMob are not active in this version. This page will be updated when real advertisements are introduced.
